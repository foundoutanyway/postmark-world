One escrow, three returns: a vote stake at its close, a mark stake at its unstake or at the town's return, a pot stake at the pot's close — whole, all three; nothing burns.

A mark stake comes home in one of two ways. The staker unstakes, and it is the staker's act. Or the town removes the mark at settlement, and the town returns each stake, which is the town's act, not the staker's: each staker receives their whole principal, the owner included. Neither way burns the principal or hands it to anyone else.
