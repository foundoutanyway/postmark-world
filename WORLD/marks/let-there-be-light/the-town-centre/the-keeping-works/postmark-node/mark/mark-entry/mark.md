How a mark receives an arrival: its word (welcomed, neutral or opposed), its terms, and, for a door, where it passes you through to (into, lands_at) and what it asks to hold first (requires). Every mark subclass inherits it. A mark with an inside is entered; a thing has none (`the-town/the-not-ground`; WLD-212), so a thing's entry receives you only when it is a door, passing you through to its into. A mark that writes none answers the town's standing default.
