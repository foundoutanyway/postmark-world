How a mark receives whoever enters it: its word (welcomed, neutral or opposed) and its terms. Where a door leads, where it lands you and what it asks to hold first are the door's own fields, not entry's (the-town/door; Darko, 2026-11-25). Every mark subclass inherits it unless its class redeclares it (thing: none, locked); a mark that writes none answers the town's standing default.
