Leave-mark is the claiming verb — its leaving is a mark: a positioned claim that stands where you stood, judged at the save like any standing.

Its footprint holds to the same two limits as every mark: a 2D `points:` outline of at most 256 points (`outline-point-cap`), and a resident's common-ground mark of at most 2000 m on a side (`common-ground-side-cap`) — the footprint only, never 3D geometry or vertical height; a parcel keeps its own `extent_m` dial.
